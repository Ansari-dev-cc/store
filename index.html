About Our Store

Welcome to NIHMA Store

"നിറയെ രുചി നിറഞ്ഞ മനസ്സ് • എന്നും മികച്ച ഗുണനിലവാരം!"

Authentic Kerala taste and trusted grocery essentials, packed fresh and served with genuine warmth.

NIHMA Store is your local neighborhood grocery in Kerala. We take pride in stocking daily farm-fresh local produce including raw and ripe Nendran bananas, fresh tapioca (kappa), sweet coconuts, shallots, curry leaves, and country vegetables.

Along with fresh vegetables, our shelves feature traditional crispy Kerala banana chips, delicious bakery bread and rusks, Palakkadan Matta rice, pure double-filtered coconut oil, and famous Neyar masalas.
